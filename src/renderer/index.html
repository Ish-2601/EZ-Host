      Share with friends

      Other addresses (this PC, your home network)

      What your friends' Minecraft needs

      Their game must match the server exactly. Otherwise Minecraft says "Connection Lost" or "missing mods".

        Give your friends the same mods

        1. Send the .zip to your friend.
        2. In the CurseForge app they open Minecraft, click Create Custom Profile, then Import, and pick the .zip.
        3. They play from that new profile.

        Joining in Minecraft

        1. Wait until the server says Running.
        2. Open Minecraft from the matching profile.
        3. Click Multiplayer, then Add Server.
        4. Paste the address from Share with friends into Server Address, then click Done.
        5. Double-click the server to join.
